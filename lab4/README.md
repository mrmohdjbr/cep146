# # Scrambled Eggs

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- Salt
- Butter

## Cooking Steps

1. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
2. Mix the eggs well.
3. Heat a pan and add butter.
4. Pour in the eggs.


6. Stir until fully cooked.

*Tip:* Use medium heat so the eggs do not burn
